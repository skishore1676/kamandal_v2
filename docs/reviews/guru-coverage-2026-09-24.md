Deterministic recognition of these shapes applies to newly interpreted or revised posts. Unchanged cached episodes are reused; the previously cached META episode remains marked incomplete in the active feed until a targeted, effect-free historical re-evaluation is reviewed. The manual image audit above records what its existing source evidence contains without treating it as a live candidate.

## Build order

1. Repair acquisition continuity and source evidence: raise the ten-post collection ceiling, recover missing public media/article terms where available, resolve edited-post lineage, and distinguish confirmed openings from research lists and proposed ideas.
2. Make model-complete distinct from independently source-verified. The deployed exact live path now requires a second image transcription to match legs and displayed price, and rechecks the current verified source revision before submission and replacement. Conflicts and multi-package ambiguity stay parked. Measure the source-verified denominator after the next natural acquisition; a second model pass is corroboration, not guaranteed image truth.
3. Promote the frequent two-leg calendars and diagonals only after a source-verified replay, exact-specific playbook windows, fresh broker preflight/BPR proof, and Kamandal close/reconciliation proof. The generic manager has natural live calendar/diagonal receipts, but those do not prove the exact guru route.
4. Add butterflies and CRABs behind shadow first, with explicit ratio and maximum-loss validation. Long calls also need single-leg quantity, entry guard, and cash-flow fixes before live permission. Keep futures options, covered calls requiring share coverage, and composites parked until their own broker and exit evidence exists.
5. Recompute occurrence-weighted understanding and live executable coverage from the repaired corpus. Expand `trade_sources.live_structures` only for structures whose full proof passes; a comma-separated name alone provides no capability.

The `portfolio_sleeves` 40/40/80 limits and `trade_sources` switches remain the operator controls. A new or lowered limit blocks entries without forcing a position exit. Kamandal continues managing positions already opened when a source route moves Off.
