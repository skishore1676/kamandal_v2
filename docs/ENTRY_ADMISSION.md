# Entry admission and Guru timing

The Sheet remains the authority for 40% current ideas, 40% exact Guru packages,
and 80% total BPR ceilings. Source freshness, supported contracts, sizing, quotes,
broker preflight, venue/account buying power, duplicate protection and health
checks remain mandatory at the existing guarded submission boundary.

A current daily owner selection supersedes older rank-one, unsubmitted plan
reservations before execution checks capital. Retirement is atomic and refuses
broker evidence, attempted submission, replacement children, or independently
staged Guru work. A terminal basket sibling is skipped; pending/active siblings
keep their cockpit authorization. Replacement children remain active commitments.

The existing X intake evaluates changed Guru evidence through `unified-plan
--guru-only`. It uses the full frozen policy and account scope, with no ideas or
market-scan groups, and stages authorized exact entries without replacing the
ideas cockpit. A revision is evaluated once per policy/day; normal scheduled
planning continues to reevaluate the full book. Staged tickets retry through the
existing executor. An independent Guru ticket cannot be retired merely because
it is absent from the ideas cockpit. A blocked Guru cannot starve that lane.

Birdclaw's existing digest owner adds correspondent-only afternoon captures;
Kamandal intake follows by one minute. Frequency rises from 15 minutes after
13:00 CT to 2–5 minutes near the close. Generic digest extraction retains its
original slots and stable publication. Extra executor ticks at 14:51–14:54 CT
follow the last capture/intake at 14:52/14:53 CT and support the Guru-only cutoff at 14:55 CT (five minutes before regular close).
Ordinary entries retain 14:40 CT. Product/early-close calendars and the opening
buffer still apply. Acquisition or interpretation latency can still miss a cutoff;
none of these schedules guarantees an entry or fill.
